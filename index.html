Who we are

We started this because the tours we saw were not the ones we would take

Most Australian itineraries are built around a coach, a car park and a clock. Ours are built around a day. We live here; we know which beach is still empty at seven in the morning, which winery will keep pouring after four, and which road is worth the extra hour.

We drive you ourselves, in small numbers, and we plan around the people in the car rather than the other way around. If the children are tired we stop; if the weather turns generous we stay another night. Neither needs a discussion.

Every trip starts with a conversation rather than a brochure. Tell us who is travelling, what they care about, and how much of the day they want to spend moving. We will build the rest.

Where we go

Each region asks for a different kind of week

Australia is larger than it looks on a page; Sydney to Perth is about the same flight as London to Istanbul. Rather than fitting everything into one trip, we usually suggest two or three regions and enough time to do them properly.

The Tropical North

Cairns, the Daintree, the Reef and Darwin. At its best in the dry, May to September.

The Red Centre

Uluru, Kata Tjuta, Kings Canyon and Alice Springs. Early starts, quiet afternoons.

The East Coast

Sydney, the Blue Mountains, the Hunter, Byron and Brisbane. Workable all year.

The South

Melbourne, the Great Ocean Road, Adelaide, Kangaroo Island and Tasmania. Summer and autumn.

The West

Perth, Margaret River and the south-west coast. For travellers with a longer holiday.

Journeys

These four routes cover most of what people come here for

These are the shapes we run most often. Combine them, split them or stretch them. They are a starting point rather than a menu.

01

The Tropical North

Cairns · Daintree · The Reef · The Whitsundays

Reef in the morning, rainforest in the afternoon. We swim from a small boat rather than a pontoon, and we walk the Daintree with a guide who grew up on that country. May to September gives you clear water and dry air.

7 to 10 days Easy pace Good from age 6
02

The Red Centre

Uluru · Kata Tjuta · Kings Canyon · Alice Springs

The desert does most of the work here. Sunrise at Kata Tjuta, the walk into Kings Canyon before the heat arrives, and a night with no artificial light for a thousand kilometres. We keep the days short and the mornings early.

4 to 6 days Early starts Cultural guiding included
03

Sydney and the East Coast

Sydney · Blue Mountains · Hunter Valley · Byron Bay

Sydney deserves more than two days, and the coast north of it deserves more than a drive-through. Harbour swims, an early ferry, the Blue Mountains on a weekday, and small Hunter wineries that never take bus groups.

6 to 9 days Any month Half city, half coast
04

The South and Tasmania

Melbourne · Great Ocean Road · Kangaroo Island · Tasmania

Cold water, good food and the best light in the country. Melbourne on foot, the Great Ocean Road driven slowly and in the right direction, then across to Tasmania for walking, oysters and empty roads.

8 to 12 days Food-led Some walking

How we work

The same people plan your trip and then drive it

1

A conversation

Twenty minutes on dates, who is coming, a budget range and what you want the trip to feel like. No sales script.

2

A draft you can argue with

A day-by-day plan, suggested accommodation and a clear price. We expect you to change it, and changes cost nothing.

3

We book it

Accommodation, domestic flights, permits, entry tickets and restaurant tables. You only need to get here.

4

We travel with you

One guide and driver for the whole trip, adjusting as the weather, the mood and the day actually go.

What the price covers

  • Private vehicle, with your guide driving
  • Accommodation we have stayed in ourselves
  • Domestic flights and all airport transfers
  • National park entry and any permits required
  • Breakfast daily, plus the meals marked in your plan
  • A pre-trip guide and someone reachable the whole way

What it does not cover

  • International flights
  • Visas and travel insurance
  • Meals and drinks outside the plan
  • Personal spending and gratuities

We quote on what the trip actually costs, with no hidden commission. If something sits outside your budget we will say so plainly.

Practical notes

The things people ask us before they commit

Group size

Six guests at most, and never combined with strangers. Multi-generation families travel in a second vehicle.

Language

English and Mandarin throughout, including your itinerary, pre-trip guide and daily briefings.

Travelling with children

We travel with our own. That means car seats, longer middays and no attractions that ask a child to stay quiet for two hours.

Pace

One or two anchors a day and the rest left open. Most people tell us afterwards that the open part was the best part.

Booking

A deposit confirms the trip, with the balance due six weeks before you fly. Cancellation terms sit on page one, not in an annex.

Changes

Weather, health and second thoughts all change plans. Rearranging days costs nothing, before or during the trip.
